# 🥇 Support HeFung

If the resources, scripts or notes on this site help you save time, thank you for your voluntary support. 🙏🙏🙏

## ☕ Why Your Support Matters

Maintaining this personal open-source site takes a lot of spare time and effort:

- **Resource curation** — Collect, verify and update useful tools, GitHub mirrors and utility scripts on a regular basis
- **Note & tutorial writing** — Document practical tips, dev notes and step-by-step guides
- **Site maintenance** — Iterate page components, fix bugs and respond to user feedback
- **Infra costs** — Cover domain, CDN and CI/CD build expenses for the site

## 📊 Ways to Support

### Financial Support

You can donate via the payment methods below. Any amount is much appreciated.

### Non-financial Support

- **Star our GitHub repo** — Give a star to the project on GitHub
- **Share with others** — Recommend this site to friends who may find the resources useful
- **Report broken links** — Let us know if any mirrors or resources stop working
- **Contribute improvements** — Submit PRs to fix typos or add new useful resources
- **Proofread notes** — Help correct and refine technical tutorials on the site

## ❤️ How Your Support Is Used

All donations will go towards:

1. **Maintenance time** — Compensate the time spent verifying resources and updating the site
2. **Infrastructure** — Cover domain, CDN and server costs
3. **Tools & services** — Pay for development tools and design resources
4. **Content updates** — Keep expanding and testing the mirror list and utility collection

## 🙏 Special Thanks

We will show our gratitude to all supporters in the project. Your name will be featured:

- In the sponsor list in the project README
- In the supporter list on this page
- In the acknowledgement section of version release notes

Thank you again for your support! Every contribution helps keep this collection of tools and resources available and up to date.

## 📘 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will I get special access or perks after sponsoring?**
A: No. All content on this site stays completely free and open. Sponsorship is purely voluntary support and does not affect your access to any resources or features.

**Q: Can I choose my own donation amount?**
A: Yes, every payment channel supports custom amounts. Any amount, large or small, is welcome and greatly appreciated.

**Q: Will my name be added to the supporter list?**
A: That is entirely up to you. If you would like to be credited, your chosen display name will be listed; otherwise, your entry will be shown anonymously or partially redacted with asterisks.

**Q: How do I report a broken link or dead mirror?**
A: Feel free to open an issue on GitHub. I will retest the link and replace it with a working alternative as soon as possible.

**Q: Do I have to sponsor to use this site?**
A: Not at all. This site will always be free for everyone. Sponsorship is just a way to say thanks for the work, not a paywall or requirement to access content.

## **🔔 Notice**: Sponsorship is completely voluntary. It will not affect your free access to any content or resources on this site.
